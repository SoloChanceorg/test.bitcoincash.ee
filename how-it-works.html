How bitcoincash.ee Pool Works

A Bitcoin Cash mining pool built like a game: every share you submit competes for a spot on the round's leaderboard, and every block pays out instantly in its own coinbase — no accounts, no waiting, no trust required.

The Game
🎮
It is more like a game, less like a pool

Most mining pools fall into one of two camps: solo pools, where you chase the full block reward alone with long odds and nothing until you win, or proportional pools, where rewards trickle in steadily but there's nothing to get excited about.

bitcoincash.ee Pool turns mining into a live scoreboard. Every share you submit is ranked by difficulty against everyone else's since the last block. Push a high enough share and you land on the Top 13 Best Shares board — and you get paid when the round ends, whether or not you're the one who actually finds the block. Find the block yourself, and you also walk away with a flat 1 BCH bonus on top. One round, two ways to win.

Every Block, 1 BCH Finder Bonus, Top 3 Big Wins, Following 10 Consolation Prizes
🧱
What Happens the Moment a Block Is Found
  1. 2% Pool Fee — taken first, straight off the full block reward (Block Reward + Tx Fees).
  2. 1 BCH Finder Bonus — paid next, directly to whoever found the block, straight in the coinbase.
  3. Whatever remains — split among the round's 13 highest-difficulty shares: 1st gets 38.3%, 2nd 23.6%, 3rd 14.6%, and 4th–13th get 2.35% each.

Everything lands as outputs in that same coinbase transaction — no pool-side ledger and no separate payout run.

Note

These figures show a plain 3.125 BCH block reward only, with no transaction fees or USD values involved — just to give an idea of the distribution. On top of that, whatever transaction fees are collected in the block will be added to the pot and split the same way, except the block finder's 1 BCH bonus, which stays a fixed 1 BCH no matter how much transaction fees add to the block.

If splitting the pot leaves a leftover dust satoshi because the amount doesn't divide evenly, the block finder receives that dust too.
