5. Payouts

Payouts are not sent as a separate transaction — they are placed directly as outputs in that same block's coinbase transaction the moment it's found, with no separate pool-side ledger or later payout transaction to trust. The payout structure is as follows:

  • The miner who finds the block receives a 1 BCH finder bonus, paid in the coinbase and spendable after 100 confirmations (~16.5 hours).
  • 2% of the remaining block reward (Block Reward + Transaction Fees − 1 BCH) is retained as the pool fee.
  • The remaining 98% is split among the round's Top 13 Best Shares — the 13 highest-difficulty shares submitted since the last block: 1st gets 38.3%, 2nd 23.6%, 3rd 14.6%, and 4th–13th get 2.35% each.

Payouts are placed automatically as coinbase outputs to the BCH address used as your mining username — no manual withdrawal, no separate transaction, and no ongoing bookkeeping. The pool does not hold funds beyond what is necessary to process the current block payout.

6. Fees

The effective fee is approximately ~1.4% of the full block reward. The 1 BCH finder bonus is paid fee-free; the 2% fee applies only to the remaining distributable reward.
