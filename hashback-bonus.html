World's first — no crypto pool has ever mined for its own miners before. We call it Hashback.

What Is Hashback Bonus?

Once an hour, bitcoincash.ee Pool points a dedicated miner at a random active address and mines for it — completely free, no strings attached.

The Idea
🔁
A Pool That Mines For You

Normally, mining only goes one direction: you point your hashrate at a pool, and the pool pays you back. Hashback Bonus flips that around — for one address every hour, the pool points its own dedicated hashrate back at a miner, for free.

As far as we know, this has never existed in crypto before. bitcoincash.ee Pool is the first pool to mine for one of its own miners, instead of the other way around.

How It Works
⏱️
Every Hour, One Lucky Address
  1. Every hour, the pool randomly selects one active address to receive the Hashback Bonus.
  2. Addresses currently on the Best 13 board are excluded from the draw — Hashback goes to someone who isn't already winning that round.
  3. A dedicated nerdQAxe++ miner is pointed at the selected address and mines for it directly for that hour.
  4. The pick is announced live in the Telegram group.
Why Not Best 13 Addresses?
🎯
Spreading It Around

Addresses already on the Best 13 board are already in line for a payout this round. Excluding them from the Hashback draw means the bonus always goes toward an active miner who isn't currently winning — keeping the odds spread across the pool instead of stacking on top of an existing lead.
