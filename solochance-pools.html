SoloChance.org Pools

The developer behind SoloChance.org runs three separate, standalone mining pools. Each one explores a different reward model — pick whichever fits how you like to mine.

From popular solo crypto mining tool to indie pool development: SoloChance.org started as one of the most popular solo mining chance calculators, then evolved into an indie crypto pool and mining-game development studio.

SoloChance.org Pool Philosophy and Constraints

All three SoloChance.org pools follow the same rules:

  1. All layer 1 coinbase payments. Every payment happens the moment a block is found — there is no custody of mined coins at any point. Every coin is mined directly to each participant's own address in that block's coinbase. Because of this, our coinbase transactions are larger than usual, so not all devices and rental platforms are fully supported. Check each pool's notes on their Connect pages.
  2. A flat 2% fee. No other hidden costs under any other name. You get exactly what you see in the UI at the moment a block is found — every value shown comes directly from the pool and node.
  3. Every pool has its own Telegram group. Join and ask whatever questions you have.
bch.ee Parasite Pool
bch.ee Parasite Pool — Bitcoin Cash (BCH) · 1 BCH Finder Bonus + Proportional Payouts
Inspiration

The very first SoloChance.org pool — inspired by the original Bitcoin Parasite Pool (parasite.space). It's a Bitcoin Cash port of the original Parasite Pool.

Standard parasite payment scheme. The block finder gets 1 full BCH, and the rest is distributed as a PROP payment scheme, proportionally by work done.

Coinbase Payout
  1. Pool Fee: 2% of the coinbase reward.
  2. Finder Bonus: 1 BCH, plus any leftover dust from the split calculations.
  3. Rest of the coinbase: proportional payments to the 100 top-earning miners. Work done by the 101st and beyond carries over to the next block.

solo100.org
solo100.org — Bitcoin (BTC) · 10% Finder Bonus + Best 100 Leaderboard
Inspiration

While developing the Best Share page of bch.ee Parasite Pool, our first original idea was born: what would happen if we gamified the whole thing and distributed the reward according to the top 100 best shares? This is our first and only Bitcoin pool, and it will change home mining once it finds its first block.

The original — the world's first Bitcoin mining meta-game. Every round, the 100 highest-difficulty shares each claim an equal slice of the block when it's found, regardless of your hashrate, work done, or accepted shares. Only your single best share counts.

Coinbase Payout
  1. Pool Fee: 2% of the coinbase reward, plus any leftover dust from the split calculations.
  2. Finder Bonus: 10% of the coinbase reward.
  3. Rest: the remaining 88% of the coinbase reward, evenly divided among the Best 100 addresses — the top 100 best shares.
First Block

solo100.org hasn't found a mainnet block yet — its first block will change the home/hobby mining space. Its first Testnet4 block was Block 134463, mined 2026-05-10 07:54:36 UTC.

bitcoincash.ee Pool — Bitcoin Cash (BCH) · 1 BCH Finder Bonus + Best 13 Fibonacci Leaderboard + Hashback Bonus
Inspiration

The natural next step: we wanted to test solo100.org's gamified mining idea on a network where blocks are much easier to find than on Bitcoin — so solo100.org and Bitcoin Cash merged into bitcoincash.ee.

This pool — the second Stratum game, and the first Bitcoin Cash mining meta-game. Instead of an equal split, the round's top 13 shares get a Fibonacci-weighted cut, biggest at 1st place. It also runs Hashback Bonus, a free hourly hashrate giveaway found on no other pool — and a world's first in its own right. It's our most sophisticated pool ever developed.

Coinbase Payout
  1. Pool Fee: 2% of the coinbase reward.
  2. Finder Bonus: 1 BCH, plus any leftover dust from the split calculations.
  3. Rest of the coinbase: the Best 13 leaderboard — the top 13 best-share addresses.
    • 🥇 1st place — 38.3% of the pot
    • 🥈 2nd place — 23.6%
    • 🥉 3rd place — 14.6%
    • 4th–13th place — 2.35% each, everyone on the board gets paid
First Block

World's first Stratum game (mining meta-game) block ever mined — Block 965765, mined 2026-08-26 14:21:37 UTC. All rewards were distributed according to the Best 13 leaderboard, ranked by each address's best share — not by total work done.
